# Reels Archetypes

Nine proven reel structures. In step 2 of the workflow, match the core idea to an archetype — the archetype dictates the **beat arc** and the **ideal length**. Pick the one whose *shape* fits the payoff, not just the topic.

Every archetype obeys the universal spine: **HOOK (0–3s) → STAVKA → QIYMAT beats → PAYOFF → LOOP/CTA**. The archetype only changes what fills the beats and how long the reel runs.

Selection cheat-sheet:
- Emotional pull, trust, a journey → **Story** or **Transformation**
- Save-worthy quick reference → **Listicle**
- "How to do X" the viewer can follow → **Tutorial**
- Reframe a belief the audience holds wrong → **Myth-bust**
- "Stop doing this" warning → **Mistakes**
- Relatability, tag-a-friend, loop → **POV / Relatable**
- Scroll-stopping curiosity → **Surprising fact**
- "This vs that", a decision → **Comparison**

Length rule of thumb: reach/new-audience archetypes stay **7–30s**; depth/existing-audience archetypes run **30–90s** — but only if every second holds.

---

## 1. Story (before → after / case)
Best for: trust, emotion, client results, personal journey. Strong send/save driver.
Ideal length: 25–60s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: the "after" or the turning point ("6 oyda 0 dan 40 ta mijozga — qanday")
- STAVKA: the "before" — the starting pain, relatable and specific
- QIYMAT beats: the turning points / what changed, in sequence (one per beat)
- PAYOFF: the after / result — honest, no inflated numbers
- LOOP/CTA: "Sizga ham shu yo'l kerakmi? Kommentga 'X'" or send-to-a-friend
Rule: honesty is load-bearing. Real results only — a fake case destroys trust faster than any other archetype.

## 2. Listicle ("N ta …")
Best for: save-worthy quick references. High save + rewatch rate.
Ideal length: 15–35s (fast).
Beat arc:
- HOOK: promise the count + the payoff ("3 ta … — 2-chisini hech kim aytmaydi")
- STAVKA: why this list matters now (one line)
- QIYMAT beats: one item per beat, each 2–3s with a quick visual + one-line explanation
- PAYOFF: the strongest item saved for last (or the "bonus")
- LOOP/CTA: "Qaysi biri yangi edi? Kommentga" / "Saqlab qo'y"
Rule: the number in the hook must match the beats. Promise 3, deliver 3. Fast pacing is the whole point.

## 3. Tutorial (how-to / bosqichma-bosqich)
Best for: a process the viewer can replicate. Very high save + return rate.
Ideal length: 20–60s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: the outcome, not the process ("30 soniyada … — mana qanday")
- STAVKA: what they need / why most get it wrong
- QIYMAT beats: Qadam 1 → 2 → 3, in order; speed through boring steps, slow on the key one
- PAYOFF: the finished result shown "in full glory"
- LOOP/CTA: keyword → DM for the full template/checklist
Rule: steps must be genuinely sequential. Show, don't just tell — demonstration holds better than narration.

## 4. Transformation (before/after / reveal)
Best for: visible contrast — draft vs final, messy vs clean, unoptimized vs refined.
Ideal length: 10–30s (short, punchy).
Beat arc:
- HOOK: tease the "after" for half a second, then cut to "before" ("Bu shu holatdan boshlangan")
- STAVKA: the state of the "before" (the pain)
- QIYMAT beats: the change happening — quick transitions, clear contrast
- PAYOFF: the full "after" reveal on the beat drop
- LOOP/CTA: loop-back to the "before" for a rewatch; or "Do'stingga yubor"
Rule: contrast + speed drive replays. The clearer the before/after gap, the more rewatches.

## 5. Myth-bust (mif buzish)
Best for: reframing a belief the audience holds. High retention (tension).
Ideal length: 20–45s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: state the myth as the viewer believes it, then "…noto'g'ri"
- STAVKA: what believing this is costing them
- QIYMAT beats: why the myth is wrong (the mechanism), then the correct model piece by piece
- PAYOFF: the one true thing to do instead
- LOOP/CTA: save or keyword → DM
Rule: attack the belief, not the viewer. "Ko'pchilik shunday o'ylaydi" > "Sen xato qilyapsan".

## 6. Mistakes (xatolar / "shuni qilma")
Best for: warnings that trigger recognition ("men ham shunday qilardim").
Ideal length: 20–40s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: name the silent mistake ("Bu … ni o'ldiryapti")
- STAVKA: the cost of the mistakes
- QIYMAT beats: Xato #1, #2, #3 — one per beat, each with the fix in one line
- PAYOFF: the single most important fix
- LOOP/CTA: keyword → DM, or "Qaysi xatoni qilgansan? Kommentga"
Rule: each mistake should sting a little (recognition) but offer the fix immediately.

## 7. POV / Relatable (loop-based)
Best for: relatability, tag-a-friend sends, replay-driven reach. Highest send potential.
Ideal length: 7–20s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: a POV frame the target instantly recognizes ("POV: mijoz tunda yozadi, sen uxlayapsan")
- STAVKA: escalate the relatable tension
- QIYMAT beats: the situation plays out (often no VO — text + acting + sound)
- PAYOFF: the punchline / twist
- LOOP/CTA: end so it loops seamlessly into the hook; CTA optional ("Tag qil o'sha odamni")
Rule: this archetype lives on *recognition* and the *seamless loop*. If it doesn't make one specific person go "bu men-ku", change the POV.

## 8. Surprising fact ("Bilarmiding…")
Best for: scroll-stopping curiosity, quick authority, shares.
Ideal length: 7–25s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: the counterintuitive fact, stated flat ("Instagram like'ni umuman sanamaydi")
- STAVKA: why this changes what the viewer thought
- QIYMAT beats: the explanation / proof, one beat at a time
- PAYOFF: the practical "so what — endi shuni qil"
- LOOP/CTA: "Buni bilmagan do'stingga yubor" (send) / save
Rule: the fact must be true and defensible. A surprising *false* fact is the fastest way to lose trust.

## 9. Comparison (this vs that)
Best for: decisions, positioning, "which should you use".
Ideal length: 15–40s.
Beat arc:
- HOOK: the matchup + stakes ("Qo'lda javob vs avtomatik DM — farqi kunni o'zgartiradi")
- STAVKA: why the choice matters for the viewer
- QIYMAT beats: side-by-side points — A vs B, split screen or quick cuts, one axis per beat
- PAYOFF: the verdict (honest — not everything wins on every axis)
- LOOP/CTA: keyword → DM, or "Sen qaysi birini ishlatasan? Kommentga"
Rule: split-screen or hard cuts between the two options keeps the eye moving; one comparison axis per beat.

## Anti-patterns (avoid in every archetype)
- **Slow intro.** "Salom, bugun men…" — the first 3s must be the hook, not a greeting. 50% leave here.
- **Padding to hit a length.** Every second earns its place or gets cut. Retention % > runtime.
- **Loop closed early.** A beat that fully resolves gives no reason to keep watching. Keep a thread pulling to the payoff.
- **Weak payoff.** A big hook with a limp ending trains viewers to skip your next reel. The payoff must deliver the hook's promise.
- **No mute layer.** No on-screen text = a dead reel for the 85% watching on mute.
- **Dead ending.** "Rahmat, obuna bo'ling" with no single action or loop. Always loop-back OR one CTA (DM keyword / send / save).
- **Reposted/watermarked footage.** Visible watermark or aggregator clips get down-ranked. Own or materially transform.
- **Fabricated numbers.** No invented "%". Reframe to an honest claim.
